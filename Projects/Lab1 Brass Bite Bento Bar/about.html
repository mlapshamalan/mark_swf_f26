The Legacy & Machinery

Discover how a decommissioned steam terminal became New London's premier culinary outpost.

From Steam Depot to Dining Command

Established in 1888 under the decree of Grand Marshal Bartholomew "Gears" Montgomery, the Brass & Bite was born from a need to feed working couriers, steam engineers, and high-ranking bulldog naval officers efficiently.

What began as a weathered Victorian transit station was overhauled with brass pressurization tanks, copper ducting, and automated heat distribution. Today, the depot stands as a monument to thermal culinary art, fusing traditional Asian bento arrangements with high-pressure steam cooking.

Pneumatic Tube Dispatch

Our signature dining feature is the Pneumatic Capsule Transit Network. Once your order is sealed inside a brass bento container in the kitchen vault, it travels through pressurized copper piping overhead directly to your dining booth table.

This ensures that your broth remains piping hot, your rice stays perfectly moist, and your meal arrives with zero heat loss during transmission.

Operational Specs

  • Pressure: 45 PSI Line Feed
  • Hold Temp: 185°F Constant
  • Delivery Speed: 12 seconds
